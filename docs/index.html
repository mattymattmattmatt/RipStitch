RipStitch

Rip reads a link and grabs the video or audio you pick, using yt-dlp through a small engine on your own computer. Stitch trims and joins clips into one cut, right here in the browser, and exports a video or a lossless FFmpeg script.

On Windows? There’s also a standalone desktop app: Rip and Stitch in their own window, nothing to install.
